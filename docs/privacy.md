# Privacy policy

Prism processes JSON and XML locally in the browser. It has no account system, analytics, advertising, telemetry endpoint, or document-upload service.

Document content is held in session memory for rendering, indexing and search. It is not stored by Prism. Supported display preferences are stored in browser extension local storage. Closing the viewer disposes its session and workers. Browser/OS clipboard and downloaded files remain under the user's control after explicit copy/download actions.

Prism does not refetch the current page to obtain XML. When exact source is unavailable, it identifies reconstructed XML. Clicking a safe HTTP(S) link opens that destination and is subject to that site's policy. The browser's own extension/store/update services operate independently of Prism.

HTTP(S) content-script access is used to detect raw JSON/XML and offer the viewer. Ordinary application pages are left untouched unless explicitly invoked. No cookies, history, or account tokens are collected.

Questions or privacy problems can be reported through the repository; security-sensitive material should use the private reporting route in SECURITY.md.
